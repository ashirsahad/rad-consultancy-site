KYC & AML Policy

Our Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) controls help us verify identity, assess risk, and comply with applicable regulations.

Identification

Government-issued ID and recent proof of address for each UBO, director and authorized signatory.

Verification

Sanctions & PEP screening, source-of-funds checks when required, and enhanced due diligence for higher risk profiles.

Monitoring

Ongoing monitoring of client activity and documentation refresh based on risk category.

Reporting

Suspicious activity reporting and record retention aligned with UAE & international standards.
